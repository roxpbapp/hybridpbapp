Privacy policy

HybridPB for Android · Last updated 23 November 2026

The short version

HybridPB has no account and no server of its own. Everything you log is stored on your phone, and HybridPB itself sends it nowhere. We do not collect, sell or share your data, and the app contains no advertising or tracking.

What the app stores

The workouts, sessions, exercises, body measurements, photos, videos and settings you enter. All of it is kept in the app's own storage on your phone.

Android backup

If backup is switched on for your phone, Android includes HybridPB's workouts, history and settings in the backup it keeps in your Google account, so they return when you set up a new phone. Google holds that backup under your Google account; HybridPB cannot see it. Photos and videos you add to exercises are left out of it. You can switch Android backup off in your phone's settings.

Backup files

When you save a backup, or switch on automatic backup, HybridPB writes a file to the folder on your phone that you choose. If you use "Send a copy", the file is handed to the app you pick, such as a cloud storage or email app, and that service then holds it under your account with them. HybridPB never reads files you have not picked.

Health Connect (optional)

Health Connect is Android's shared store for health and fitness data. HybridPB uses it only if you allow it, and only for the following:

  • Writing: your finished training sessions, bodyweight and body-fat entries, and an estimate of active calories if you switch that on, so other apps you use can show them.
  • Reading bodyweight: entries from other apps, such as smart scales, so you do not have to type them twice.
  • Reading runs: running sessions and their distance recorded by other apps, such as a watch's app, from about the last 30 days. They are added to your history on your phone.

Data read from Health Connect stays on your phone. It is not sent to us or to anyone else, and it is not used for advertising. You can turn each of these off in the app's Settings, and revoke access at any time in Health Connect's own settings.

Files you import

If you import a CSV file, or an activity export from Garmin or Strava, HybridPB reads the file you pick and adds its sessions to your history on your phone. The file is not uploaded anywhere.

RoxPB

When you open or share a RoxPB result link, HybridPB reads the result from the link itself. Opening a result on roxpb.com sends the link to your browser; the result data sits after the "#" in the link and is not sent to the server.

Deleting your data

You can delete any session, exercise or measurement in the app. Uninstalling HybridPB removes everything it stored on your phone. Backup files you saved, and any Android backup in your Google account, are yours to delete from wherever they are kept.

Children

HybridPB is not directed at children under 13.
